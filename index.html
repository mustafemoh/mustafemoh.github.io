About

Economic analysis in service of national policy

I work on the questions that decide whether a fragile economy can finance its own development — how revenue is raised, how public resources are allocated, and how households, remittances and finance connect to growth.

I am currently Development Coordination Officer (Economist) at the UN Resident Coordinator's Office in Somalia, where I lead the analytical agenda of the Economic Transformation Pillar under the UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework 2026–2030, and support Somalia's Integrated National Financing Framework alongside the Ministries of Finance and Planning, the World Bank, the IMF and the AfDB.

Previously I served in the Office of the President as Deputy Development Lead for Somalia Centennial Vision 2060 — the country's first sovereign long-term development strategy — and as Senior Economist at the National Economic Council, where I co-authored the annual State of the Somali Economy report and contributed to the analytical work underpinning Somalia's HIPC completion. My academic research on financial inclusion, remittances and household welfare has been published in peer-reviewed development economics journals.

Focus areas

What I work on

Macro-fiscal analysis

Long-term fiscal projections, scenario modelling and macro-fiscal frameworks in fragile and post-HIPC settings.

National development planning

Long-term strategy, results frameworks, and translating vision documents into costed, monitorable plans.

Financing for development

INFF design, domestic revenue mobilisation, ODA analysis, diaspora bonds and blended finance.

Financial inclusion & remittances

Access to finance, household welfare effects of remittances, and financial sector deepening.

Trade & regional integration

Trade in services, AfCFTA implementation, and Somalia's integration into regional markets.

Applied econometrics

Panel data, propensity score matching, ordered response models, PCA and quantile regression.
